Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Information we collect

Information you give us

If you contact us through the Site, we collect your name, company, email address, phone number, the service you’re interested in, and the message you write. If you email or call us, we keep the information you share with us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the Site and the services that host it may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit, and the date and time of your visit.

Please don’t send sensitive information through the Site

Do not send Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, medical or health information, or insurance claim details through the contact form or by regular email. If we need that kind of information to help you, we’ll arrange a secure way to collect it.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services and the Site.
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

Cookies and third-party services

We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell your personal information. The Site loads some content from other companies, which may receive your IP address and may set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the Site.
  • Google Maps provides the map on our Contact page.
  • Our hosting provider delivers the Site and keeps standard server logs.
  • A form-delivery service passes messages sent through our contact form to us.

These companies’ use of information is governed by their own privacy policies. If we add analytics or other tools in the future, we’ll update this policy.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Site and respond to you (such as hosting, email, and form delivery), when the law requires it, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business. We do not sell your personal information.

Benefits services and sensitive information

Information we receive while providing employee benefits services, such as enrollment, claims, or plan data, is handled under our agreements with clients and applicable laws, including HIPAA where it applies. This policy covers the Site only.

How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes described above, or as the law requires.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you sent us through the Site, subject to legal requirements. Residents of some states have additional privacy rights, and we’ll honor valid requests as applicable law requires. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

The Site is intended for business and adult audiences and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other websites

The Site links to carrier and other third-party websites. We don’t control those sites and aren’t responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
